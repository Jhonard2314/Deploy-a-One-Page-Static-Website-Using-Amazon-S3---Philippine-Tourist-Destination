Welcome to Palawan

Palawan is a long, narrow island province in the western Philippines, often voted one of the most beautiful islands in the world. Its clear waters, dramatic karst cliffs and rich marine life make it a favorite for island hopping, diving and eco-adventures.

Why Visit Palawan?

Stunning Nature

Hidden lagoons, secret beaches and jungle-covered mountains.

Palawan has limestone karst, mangroves, waterfalls and rainforest, much of it protected.

Featured Attractions

Underground River

A boat ride through an 8.2 km cave system in Puerto Princesa Subterranean River National Park.

Coron

Honda Bay

Easy island hopping and snorkeling near Puerto Princesa city.

Travel Tips

  • Best time: November to May (dry season).
  • Getting there: Fly to Puerto Princesa (PPS), El Nido (ENI) or Busuanga/Coron (USU).
  • Book early: Underground River permits and peak-season tours sell out.
  • Bring cash: ATMs are limited outside the main towns; expect environmental fees.
  • Be reef-friendly: Use reef-safe sunscreen and never touch the corals.
  • Pack: Dry bag, water shoes, light clothes and a rain jacket.
